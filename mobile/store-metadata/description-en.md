## Full description (App Store / Google Play: 4000 chars)

```
Tap-tournament game. One vs one: which one feels closer?

Each theme has 8, 16, 32, or 64 contestants. They come in pairs.
You tap the one you like — the loser drops out. At the end you
have one #1, #2, #3.

After the tournament the app builds your taste portrait — a short
text based on what you actually picked. Not generic; specifically
about your set.

— 112 themes across 17 life-domain clusters
— ~1900 items inside the themes
— Short tournaments (5 min) and long ones (30+ min) — pick the depth
— Grand tournaments up to 64 contestants for the long-form mood
— Full history of every tournament in your profile
— Light / dark theme, RU + EN interface
— Submit your own theme — moderators review and publish

Themes include:
— Films, TV series, anime, animation
— Music, artists, full eras
— Food, drinks, travel, cities
— Games, tech, brands, cars
— Internet vibes, aesthetics, memes
— Professions, career, philosophy
— Relationships, character, life values
— And many more

No signup required to start — just open and play.
Want to save history, add friends and compare taste? Create an
account — Sign in with Apple, Google, or email/password.

Privacy under your control:
— No analytics by default, no marketing emails by default
— Granularly toggle each consent in your profile
— Download all your data with one tap
— Delete your account permanently anytime
— Details: isverifiedby.me/privacy.html
```
